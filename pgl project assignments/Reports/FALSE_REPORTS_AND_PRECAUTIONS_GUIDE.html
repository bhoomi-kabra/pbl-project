🛡️ Anti-Fraud & False Report Precautions Guide

Nashik Roads & Civic Monitor: Integrity Protocol, Current Code Safeguards & Viva Q&A

IN SIMPLE WORDS: If people upload fake pictures from Google or report non-existent potholes, municipal teams waste money and time. This document explains what our software currently does, how to see it in the demo, the 5 future industry precautions, and the exact words to say to Sir during viva.

1. What False Reports Are & Why It Matters

Municipal civic apps face 4 types of fake submissions:

  • Downloaded / Google Photos: A user downloads an old pothole photo from the web and reports it in Nashik.
  • Duplicate Reports: 10 different people report the same pothole on Gangapur Road, creating 10 duplicate work orders.
  • Out-of-City Submissions: A user in Mumbai or Pune files a complaint inside Nashik's database.
  • Contractor Fraud: A contractor marks a road as "Resolved" without actually repairing the road.

2. Current Safeguards Built into Our Code (Show These in Demo)

1. Mandatory Legal Declaration Checkbox (Section 396 MMC Act)

Inside the complaint modal (ComplaintFormModal.tsx), citizens must check a declaration agreeing that submitting fake or downloaded pictures is punishable by law under Section 396 of the Maharashtra Municipal Corporation Act & IT Act 2000.

2. Exact Landmark Snapping & GPS Pinpoint

Citizens cannot type vague or out-of-city locations. Typing "Hirawadi Road" auto-snaps coordinates to [20.0270, 73.8140] and offers official spot tags in Panchavati.

3. Community Peer Review: "Flag as False Report" Button

In the Citizen Feed (SocialMediaFeed.tsx), every card has a 🚩 Flag as False Report button. If neighbors see an inaccurate report, they flag it. The post immediately displays a red alert banner: "⚠️ Community Alert: Suspected False or Duplicate" and moves to the "Flagged for Review" tab for the Ward Officer.

4. "Closed != Resolved" Contractor Anti-Fraud Engine

In VerificationTracker.tsx, municipal contractors are blocked from closing tickets unilaterally. They must upload an "After Repair" photo, and only the citizen reporter has the digital authority to confirm and officially close the ticket.

3. The 5-Pillar Industry Solution (Phase-2 Roadmap)

Pillar Technology How It Stops False Reports in Simple Words
1. EXIF Camera Metadata Lock EXIF.js Parser Extracts hidden GPS and timestamp from photo file. Rejects screenshots and downloaded Google photos that lack camera hardware tags.
2. Spatial Proximity Clustering PostGIS ST_DWithin(20m) If a new complaint is filed within 20 meters of an existing pothole, it auto-merges it into a "+1 Upvote" instead of creating a duplicate ticket.
3. Mobile OTP Verification SMS Gateway (Fast2SMS / Twilio) Sends 4-digit SMS OTP to verify mobile numbers and limits each phone to max 3 complaints/day to prevent bot spam.
4. Perceptual Image Hashing (pHash) ImageHash Algorithm Generates an image fingerprint. Detects and blocks the exact same photo reused across multiple complaints.
5. Civic Karma Trust Score Reputation Algorithm Citizens with confirmed genuine reports gain trust score; accounts with repeatedly flagged false reports get quarantined.

4. Professor Viva Defense Guide (Exact Words to Say to Sir)

❓ Sir asks: "What if someone uploads a downloaded photo of a pothole from Google?"
💡 Answer: "Sir, currently we require a mandatory legal declaration under Section 396 MMC Act, and local residents can click 'Flag as False Report' to mark the post for inspection. In Phase-2, we have designed an EXIF metadata scanner that checks camera hardware tags and live GPS coordinates inside the JPEG file, automatically rejecting downloaded images."
❓ Sir asks: "What if 10 people report the exact same road damage?"
💡 Answer: "Sir, in our feed we have a '+1 I Face This' button. In our database roadmap, we use Spatial Proximity Clustering (within 20 meters), which automatically links nearby complaints to the primary ticket and converts them into upvotes instead of creating duplicate repair orders."
❓ Sir asks: "What if a contractor claims they fixed the road, but did nothing?"
💡 Answer: "Sir, this is our core innovation: 'Closed != Resolved'. Contractors cannot close a complaint unilaterally. They must upload an 'After Repair' photo, and the ticket enters PENDING_VERIFICATION. Only the original citizen reporter has the digital sign-off authority to confirm and officially close the ticket."
